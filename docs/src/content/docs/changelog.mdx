### v2.3.9
- Feat: The audio player has a **Fixed Headroom** switch. With it on, the player plays 12 dB below full level instead of following the automatic preamp, so changing one band no longer changes the volume of everything else. An EQ that boosts more than 12 dB still plays at the lower preamp it needs. It is off by default and affects the player only. See [Fixed headroom](./guide-for-users/equalizing.mdx#fixed-headroom). (#148)

### v2.3.8
- Fix: AutoEQ's **Gain Range** now limits each band on its own, as in CrinGraph. Before, its maximum also capped the boost of the whole EQ. In **Exact match**, bands can now stack past the range where a curve falls far below the target, usually a treble that rolls off, so some fits boost more and get a lower preamp. **Treble-safe** aims for at most 6 dB of boost across the whole EQ, AutoEq's own limit, whatever the range says. Graphic EQ presets follow the same rules. If a fit gets too quiet, lower the range's maximum or switch to Treble-safe. See [Fit mode](./features/equalizer.mdx#fit-mode).
- Fix: Hardware EQ now also warns that a device may clip when its preamp can't go as low as the bands' boost needs. Before, it warned only for devices that take no preamp at all.

### v2.3.7
- Feat: Hardware EQ now shows a device's own settings next to **Device EQ**: headroom, crossfeed, DAC filter, output gain, USB mode and more, depending on the device. A setting applies as soon as you change it. If a setting restarts the device, the panel asks first and reconnects afterwards. See [Device settings](./features/device-peq.mdx#device-settings).
- Feat: A device with more than one EQ, such as the Qudelix 5K with its user EQ and headphone EQ ("SPK EQ"), gets an **EQ** row. It picks the EQ that **Device EQ**, **Read**, **Write**, auto-write and the band list's limits use. See [Devices with more than one EQ](./features/device-peq.mdx#devices-with-more-than-one-eq).
- Feat: On a device that keeps its presets as a list, such as the Topping DX5 II and DX9 Discrete, **Device EQ** lists the presets under their names on the device. **New**, **Rename** and **Delete** buttons add to that list and change it. See [Adding, renaming and deleting presets](./features/device-peq.mdx#adding-renaming-and-deleting-presets).
- Feat: A device with a separate set of bands for each ear, such as the Qudelix headphone EQ and the Topping DX5 II, now gets the full per-channel EQ. **Write** sends each ear the L+R bands plus that ear's own bands, **Read** brings back both ears, and the **Channel** switch stays available. Other devices still get the L+R bands only. See [Left and right](./features/device-peq.mdx#left-and-right).
- Feat: The [uniPEQ link](./features/unipeq-link.mdx#while-linked) now carries all of the above for a device connected in uniPEQ: left- and right-only bands, the device's preset list, its settings and its EQs. This needs a recent uniPEQ. With an older one, the link works as before.
- Feat: When a phone's `variants[]` names every measurement in its `file` (and `hptfs`), the variant picker now follows the order of `variants[]`, and its first entry is the default curve. A `variants[]` that names only some of them keeps the old order. The browser console warns about a phone that has `variants` but no `file`, and about a phone whose first variant isn't the first entry of `file`, because squig.link and CrinGraph would open a different one. See [Sample Sets](./guide-for-admins/manage-data.mdx#sample-sets).
- Fix: The [phone_book.json Editor](/phone-book-editor) now exports the phone-level `file` and `suffix` alongside `variants[]`, and no longer warns on import that it would drop them. A phone without `file` was missing from squig.link's cross-site search, along with every phone after it in the same file, and CrinGraph couldn't read it. If the Editor converted your `phone_book.json` before, export it again.
- Fix: Some devices report that their EQ has changed since a preset was loaded. A Qudelix does this after any edit, including one made in its own app. These devices now show **Unsaved EQ** under **Device EQ**, and **Read** and **Write** use the EQ the device is playing. Before, the panel showed the first preset instead, **Read** was unavailable, and **Write** saved over that preset. See [Device EQ](./features/device-peq.mdx#device-eq).
- Fix: Hardware EQ's controls now keep keyboard focus while the device is busy, instead of losing it. A setting slider released during a write is applied once the device is free.
- Fix: Over the uniPEQ link, pressing a button again while uniPEQ is still busy no longer causes an error notification. The status line no longer shows an old error or setting message after uniPEQ writes.
- Fix: Over the uniPEQ link, the Equalizer no longer says the preamp was auto-reduced when uniPEQ's fixed preamp is in use. Instead, it warns for each channel when that preamp leaves less headroom than the bands' boost.
- Fix: A band's gain now shows at most two decimals and its Q at most three, so a Q read from a device shows as `0.707` instead of `0.70703125`.
- Fix: The tone generator now glides between slider positions instead of stepping. Below 150 Hz it moves in 0.1 Hz steps, so every slider position changes the pitch. A move of more than an octave still jumps.

### v2.3.6
- Feat: The runs of a sample set are now drawn in graded shades of the curve's color, the first run darkest, instead of all in one color. Recoloring the curve recolors its runs too. They stay faint behind the average, and are drawn solid once the average is hidden. See [Sample selector](./guide-for-users/working-with-curves.mdx#sample-selector-for-devices-measured-more-than-once). (#134)
- Feat: Hardware EQ is updated to eqcaps 0.6, which adds support for Topping devices and more.
- Refactor: AutoEQ is updated to turboEQ 0.3.1, which is up to twice as fast and fixes a rare error that occurred during exact-match peaking-only runs.
- Fix: A fractional `reviewScore` such as `4.3` now fills exactly that much of the five-star row. Before, it was rounded down to the nearest half star, and the half star was an emoji drawn in its own size and color. (#135)

There are skip links to the controls and the graph, the divider between the graph and the panel moves with the arrow keys, each curve's buttons are announced with the curve's name, and the site selector has a filter field and arrow-key navigation. Every color role keeps 4.5:1 against its `-content`, secondary text keeps 7:1, and a `prefers-contrast: more` block raises the light theme's colors to 7:1. Two tokens are new: `--color-scrim` (the veil behind dialogs, the mobile menu and the tour) and `--color-rating` (rank stars). An existing `theme.css` keeps its own colors and works without the new tokens. See [Customizing Page Styles](./guide-for-admins/customize-page.mdx#customizing-page-styles-themecss).

- Feat: Added `TOPBAR.TITLE.ALT`, the site's name as plain text, for screen readers when the title is an image or HTML. The sample `config.js` title is now a `<span>`: don't use heading tags (`<h1>`–`<h6>`) in an HTML title. See [TOPBAR documentation](./guide-for-admins/customize-page.mdx#topbar).
- Feat: A CDN deployment can now be pinned to one exact release with `CDN_MODE.VERSION`. With `BOOT_INTEGRITY` as well, it only starts from files whose hashes are written in your own `index.html` and `config.js`. Unpinned sites now also check each script and stylesheet of a release against its hash. See [Pinning a release](./guide-for-admins/deployment/cdn.mdx#pinning-a-release).
- Feat: Every release now ships recommended security headers, a Content-Security-Policy among them, switched off: as commented lines in `.htaccess`, and as a commented `<meta>` tag in the `index.html` of the CDN release and the GitHub Pages template. Trusted Types can be added on top. See [Security Headers](./guide-for-admins/deployment/security-headers.mdx).
- Feat: The bundled `.htaccess` now sets cache lifetimes and compression on Apache. See [Caching and compression](./guide-for-admins/deployment/prebuilt.mdx#caching-and-compression).
- Feat: Hardware EQ now checks a network device's address before connecting (an IP address or a `.local` name, with an optional port), connects on Enter, and tells you when the browser can't reach the device. See [Network devices](./features/device-peq.mdx#network-devices).
- Feat (squig-link): Analytics now reports to squig.link's shared Tag Manager container with no ID needed, and `ANALYTICS_MEASUREMENT_IDS` also accepts `GTM-` containers of your own. Visitors get a **Usage statistics** switch in the Misc panel, a browser that sends Global Privacy Control loads no analytics, and the new `ANALYTICS_CONSENT` sets what Consent Mode starts from. See [Analytics and consent](./features/squiglink-integration.mdx#analytics-and-consent).
- Fix (squig-link): Fixed an issue where analytics events aren't properly sent to Google Tag Manager.
- Fix (squig-link): Fixed an issue where shop link's sale price weren't properly shown in certain conditions.
- Fix: An uploaded measurement used as the EQ source is now dimmed while you equalize, like any other source. (#115)
- Fix: A new EQ curve now starts at its source's Y-offset, and the band handles on the graph stay on the curve when it has an offset. (#116)
- Fix: Changing the language now keeps the selected devices, targets and EQ, also with `URL.AUTO_UPDATE_URL` off. (#119)
- Fix: Holding a Y-offset button now makes one undo step instead of one per tick.
- Fix: A damaged share link no longer stops the page from loading. The parts that can't be read are skipped.
- Fix: `reviewLink`, `shopLink` and `links[]` URLs in `phone_book.json` now keep query parameters such as `&tab=` and `&lt=`. A `reviewLink` or `shopLink` that isn't an `http(s)` address is ignored.
- Fix: Cross-site search no longer fails when the main index stalls but a mirror answers. It skips malformed rows instead of stopping, and a load that fails is reported instead of loading forever.
- Fix: Databases that were unreachable now carry an **Unverified** badge in the site selector, with a note under the list, instead of being dimmed.
- Fix: Error notifications now stay until dismissed, and a repeated one shows once. Dialogs and popovers taller than the screen scroll inside instead of being cut off.
- Fix: When the browser blocks copying, the share button now shows the link so you can copy it yourself.
- Fix: Uploading now refuses a file over 5 MB, or more than 40 files at once, with a message instead of trying to read them.
- Fix: The Enter or Escape that finishes typing with an IME (Korean, Japanese, Chinese) no longer also submits or cancels the field.
- Refactor: A screenshot taken in dark mode now uses the light colors of your `theme.css` instead of built-in ones.
- Refactor: Faster startup. The Equalizer panel now loads on demand, and the hover readout, Y-axis drag and cross-site search do less work.

- Feat: History & Compare now has **History** and **Bookmarks** tabs. Bookmark an EQ to keep it, delete single history entries, and add an entry to the graph as a ghost trace. History also records edits made while another panel is open. See [History & Compare](./features/equalizer.mdx#history-and-compare).

Configured by the new `PREFERENCE_BOUND.BOUND_LIST`, which replaces `BASE_DF_TARGET_FILE` — that key still works. See [Preference Bound](./features/preference-bound.mdx#offering-more-than-one-bound) and [PREFERENCE_BOUND documentation](./guide-for-admins/customize-page.mdx#preference_bound).

- Fix: A channel picked on the EQ curve now stays picked when the curve rebuilds.
- Fix: The graph inspection toggle now starts in sync with the graph's state.
